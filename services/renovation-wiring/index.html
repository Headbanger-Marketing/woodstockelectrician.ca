Electrical decisions belong early in the renovation

A renovation changes how the space will be used and what equipment it must serve. Provide the drawings and appliance specifications before the electrical scope is finalized. A Licensed Electrical Contractor can assess retained wiring, new circuits and the proposed loads. Municipal building permissions and ESA electrical notification are separate matters, and the project may require both.

Prepare the scope for your Woodstock property

Gather the appliance list with model sheets, mark where the cooktop, oven, dishwasher and refrigerator will stand, and note how far the panel is from the kitchen and what rooms lie between.

Ask the contractor which walls and ceilings must be open for new cable, who schedules that against the other trades, and whether the route could be shortened by moving an appliance.

What should the assessment establish?

Once the kitchen refit in Woodstock and its new appliance list have been assessed, you decide whether the electrical work goes ahead. The quote ties each appliance to a circuit and traces that circuit's path from the panel. Existing wiring is marked as kept or replaced, with the reason. Where the route crosses other rooms or a basement, the scope describes how openings are made good. The contractor confirms that the appliance list in the quote matches the one you supplied. The ESA notification and any building permission appear as separate steps, each with its own owner.

Ontario electrical safety

ESA says almost all electrical work needs a notification of work. If you hire someone for home electrical work, use a Licensed Electrical Contractor. Agree who files the notification and request the Certificate of Acceptance after ESA review.

Read the current ESA notification and inspection guidance, contractor hiring guidance. You can verify an electrical contractor with ESA before agreeing to work.

Questions before you agree to work

Does a new appliance list change how far the wiring has to run?

It can, because each appliance has a position and the route from the panel to that position sets how much wiring and opening work is involved. Give the contractor the layout before the cabinets are fixed.

When should the electrical scope be settled in a kitchen project?

Before walls are closed and cabinets ordered, because the route and the opening work depend on it. A scope settled late may force changes to finished surfaces, so ask for a written scope early.
